Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

In short: Tanda is a free game. It keeps only what it needs to save your farm and run the leaderboard. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can play without giving us any personal information at all.

This policy covers the game Tanda at tanda.gajananrathod.in, made by Gajanan Rathod ("we", "us"). It explains what we collect, why, where it's kept, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
A random player ID and a recovery codeWhen you first open the gameSo your farm can be saved and continued, even without signing in. Neither contains anything about you.
Your farmAs you playFields, crops, money, land, bulls, loans, missions, reputation and a 14-day ledger of your in-game trades. This is the game's save.
A leaderboard name, if you set oneIf you type one in, or when you sign in with Google (your first name, if you hadn't chosen a name)Shown publicly on the leaderboard with your in-game wealth, title and missions completed. You can change it at any time.
Your email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account ID and nameOnly if you choose "Save your farm" and sign inTo link your farm to you so you can continue on any device. We ask Google only for your basic profile and email.
Anonymous visit statisticsWhen pages loadVercel Web Analytics counts page views, countries and device types in aggregate. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
Standard server logsOn each requestOur host keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and time) for security and to fix errors.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or anything from your device beyond what's listed here, and we never ask for payment details. The game has no in-game purchases: its money is pretend and has no real value.

Stored on your device

The game keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: the key that opens your farm on this device, your settings (sound, view distance, graphics quality) and which story cards you've already seen. These never leave your device except the key, which is sent to our server to load your farm. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. Without signing in, your recovery code is then the only way back to your farm.

Where it's kept, and who helps us

  • Vercel hosts the game and its server, and provides the anonymous analytics.
  • Supabase stores the game's database and handles sign-in (Google and email links). Your email and Google account ID are held in Supabase Auth.
  • Google confirms who you are if you choose "Continue with Google". Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

These providers process data only to run the game for us. Their servers may be outside India.

What's public

The leaderboard is public. It shows your leaderboard name (or "Farmer" and four characters of your player ID), your in-game wealth and what it's made of, your title, and how many missions you've completed. It never shows your email, your player ID in full, or your recovery code.

How long we keep it

Your farm is kept while you keep playing. Farms that nobody has opened for 24 months may be deleted. When you ask us to delete your farm or account, we remove it within 30 days, apart from short-lived logs, which expire on their own.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without signing in. Signing in is optional.
  • Change or remove your leaderboard name at any time, from the leaderboard.
  • Sign out of a device from "Save your farm". Your farm stays safe in your account.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data, or ask any question about this policy, by opening an issue at github.com/gajanansr/tanda-an-village/issues. Include your recovery code or signed-in email so we can find your farm. Don't post your recovery code publicly: say you'd like to be contacted privately, and we'll reply.

These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, if you're in the EU or UK, under the GDPR.

Children

Tanda is a family-friendly game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children can play as guests, which needs no personal information. If you believe a child has signed in with their email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Only our server can read the database, device keys are stored only as hashes, and sign-in tokens are checked with Supabase before they're trusted. No system is perfectly secure, so treat your recovery code like a key.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll also mention it in the game.
