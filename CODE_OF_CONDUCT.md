# Code of Conduct

Tanda is set in a real village and draws on the life of the Banjara community. Everyone is welcome
here, and we ask everyone to behave in a way that honours that.

## Our pledge

We will make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
caste, community, tribe, religion, language, region, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age,
body, appearance, or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Talk about the Banjara people, Ukhali, and any community or faith with respect. Their mandirs, the
  pir, their festivals and their clothes appear in the game with respect.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## What we won't accept

- Casteist, communal, religious, racist, sexist or otherwise demeaning remarks or content.
- Mocking stereotypes of any community, in code, art, text or discussion.
- Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or sharing someone's private information.
- Sexualised content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may block people
who repeatedly or seriously do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer,
[@gajanansr](https://github.com/gajanansr), privately through GitHub. Reports will be kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
